## Quantities and replenishment

- **Shared category budgets:**20g Nuts/day, 7g Morning Seeds +7g Dinner Seeds/day, one Berry and one Whole Fruit choice/day. Alternative packs replace those servings; they do not create additional daily portions.
- **Subscription-first rotation:** combine compatible products into the longest useful automatic delivery cycle. Bioshi offers 7/14/21/28/30/60/90-day presets and requires one shared interval per subscription order; 90days suits pantry goods;28days suits the selected fresh rotation. Checked choices remain the macro reference day, not daily purchase mandates. Carryover reduces future quantities rather than increasing portions.
- **Morning Seeds:** Flax200g every 3months and Chia200g every90days each supply28.6 servings. Hemp200g supplies another28.6; its next pack remains a stock-based purchase because no suitable organic hulled subscription was verified. These three packs cover85.7 Seed days combined, so replenish Hemp against the remaining category stock rather than assuming all Seeds last90days exactly. Follow delivered storage instructions and grind Flax shortly before use.
- **Nuts:** the shared90-day subscription contains Walnuts600g, Pistachios450g, Almonds350g and Macadamias400g: **1.8kg =90×20g servings**. The respective budgets are30,22.5,17.5 and20 chosen days; Walnuts cover one-third of the cycle. The ordering remains evidence-weighted in the README; these are purchasing proportions, with partial-pack carryover between cycles.
- **Dinner Seeds:** Pumpkin350g + HeyBio Sunflower250g are both selected every90days. Together they supply85.7×7g servings; the remaining4.3days need30g from existing stock or the next tolerated Seed rotation. While Tahini is unavailable, top up Sunflower when needed; when restored, reduce the next Pumpkin/Sunflower quantity. Do not treat either pack as daily use or the combined600g as a full630g cycle.
- **Oat pack alignment:**2×1kg +1×300g of the same Bio Planet gluten-free Rolled Oats are selected every90days. They supply92 days at25g/day, leaving a50g delivery buffer. If this carryover accumulates unchanged, omit one300g pack after six cycles. The300g pack is ordered from supplier stock, with dispatch shown Tuesday; checkout estimates delivery Wednesday16September.
- **Pulses and Quinoa:** Beluga400g, Chickpeas400g and Black Beans500g are selected every90days. At an illustrative2.4× cooked yield they provide about14.8,19.2 and20 meals:54 combined, a small2.5-meal buffer over4/week. Keep the README's2–4 Pulse meals/week, weigh actual cooked yield and carry leftovers forward; reduce/defer a line if eating fewer Pulses rather than increasing intake to empty the packs. Quinoa500g replaces the former1kg Lunch pack.
- **Seasonings:** Oregano40g supplies80 chosen meal servings; Rosemary30g60, Thyme20g40, Cumin30g30 and Nigella50g50. These five90-day subscriptions support the existing CHOOSE ANY pattern. They do not require finishing every pack on schedule; reduce or defer an underused line.
- **Fresh food:** fresh products are the default, preferably organic. Frozen seasonal Bilberries, Blackcurrants, Tart Cherries and Brussels Sprouts remain fallbacks where fresh Gdańsk stock was not verified. Spinach's displayed use-by is 17 September; Arugula 18 September; Eggs and Kefir 24 September. Other produce uses the retailer's daily-freshness guarantee or delivered label. Replenish small fresh rotations rather than buying every alternative together.
- **Fish:** use the README's 300–450g cooked/ready-to-eat weekly rhythm, including at least 200g Fatty Fish. Count Lunch Pollock too. Frozen/preserved Fish can wait for later weeks; do not combine every Fish pack into the first week.
- **Turkey/Salmon:** Turkey500g gives three raw 150g portions plus 50g; the existing frozen seafood packs cover later alternative Lunches. Pay the 30.99zł chilled delivery rather than buying five Turkey packs solely for free shipping; use or freeze any portion before its delivered use-by. Preferred skinless Auchan Salmon is a nominal 500g weighed portion: three 150g raw servings plus about 50g. It is sold thawed: keep at 0–2°C, follow the delivered use-by and do not refreeze. Purchase when available; skin-on Salmon is excluded.
- **Free delivery:** only buy useful stock to reach a threshold. Two Matcha pouches cover 100 days and two Phileos bottles roughly 68 days; these are deliberate exceptions. No extra pantry or spice filler was added to reach 299–300zł.
