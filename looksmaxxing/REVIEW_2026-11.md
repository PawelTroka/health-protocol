# Looksmaxxing review — November 2026

**Reviewed: 2026-11-14.** The [README](../README.md#6-looksmaxxing) remains the daily protocol. This review covers the whole Looksmaxxing section and distinguishes implemented proposals from options requiring a treatment decision. Evidence, product availability and development status are a dated snapshot.

## Priorities

1. **Cheek/nose pores and texture:** choose one properly identified resurfacing course, with fractional 1064nm picosecond laser added to the comparison with ResurFX 1565nm and selected fractional CO2. Match the choice to downtime, skin type and the actual texture problem.
2. **Consistent skin quality:** retain UVMune 400 and the existing moisturizers, add outdoor lip SPF, and review whether a more tolerable retinoid formulation could improve long-term use. Current prescription doses/frequency stay unchanged.
3. **Hair density:** assess the combined oral/scalp/brow/beard plan with photographs/trichoscopy before introducing a stronger compound. Keep the established oral core and gentle haircare.
4. **Smile:** daily fitted interdental cleaning, the correctly identified toothpaste and a restoration-aware polishing/whitening plan offer concrete improvements.
5. **Procedure maintenance:** repeat treatments for a demonstrated residual target and previous benefit. A date on the calendar does not establish another course is needed.

These priorities reflect the stated pore, fine-line, under-eye and hair goals; they are not a diagnosis or a numerical prediction of improvement. Take comparable photographs at baseline and the review point, with consistent lighting, expression, camera distance and haircut. Record downtime and tolerance alongside appearance.

## Skin products and next options

### Keep and improve the core

**UVMune 400 Invisible Fluid SPF50+ stays.** Apply generously to exposed areas; reapply about every 2h outdoors and after swimming/sweating. Shade/clothing complement it. A randomized trial supports regular sunscreen use against photoaging; this does not prove one modern brand is superior. Oil Control is a possible finish change for bothersome shine, not a necessary protection upgrade. A tinted version is an optional cosmetic way to even skin tone if its shade suits. [Application guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/shade-clothing-sunscreen/how-to-apply-sunscreen) · [Photoaging trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23732711/)

**Add Avène lip SPF50+ 3g outdoors.** It has a transparent finish and no added fragrance; keep Cicaplast Lèvres/Cicalfate for comfort and overnight. Reapply during exposure and after eating/drinking. The improvement is lip coverage, with ordinary Polish pharmacy access. [Manufacturer](https://www.eau-thermale-avene.pl/p/pomadka-do-ust-spf-50-3282770204797-808759b4) · [DOZ](https://www.doz.pl/p/avene-pomadka-do-ust-spf-50-3-g-164898)

**CeraVe moisturizer, gentle cleansing and the current eye/body/hand emollients remain good practical choices.** The facial cream already contains niacinamide, ceramides and hyaluronic acid; an additional high-strength niacinamide serum has no established added benefit in this exact routine. The manufacturer does not specify its niacinamide percentage. Use Hydrating Cleanser during dryness; Blemish Control is a salicylic-acid option, not a guaranteed pore-shrinking cleanser. Moisturize dry body/hands after washing. [CeraVe formula](https://www.cerave.pl/produkty/nawilzajacy-krem-do-twarzy)

**Theramid Copper Peptide 3% remains optional.** The manufacturer identifies Copper Tripeptide-1 and markets 3% copper peptides with 13% other peptides. The current formula also lists retinyl palmitate and ascorbic derivatives; older retail ingredient lists differ. Match the delivered bottle before replenishing. This is a multi-ingredient cosmetic, with no verified finished-product superiority over the existing core. Optional SNAP-8 need not become another compulsory daily layer. [Current Theramid formula](https://nichebeautylab.com/en/products/copper-peptide)

[Tretinoin concentration trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7544967/) · [Polish Skinoren label](https://rejestry.ezdrowie.gov.pl/api/rpl/medicinal-products/10628/characteristic) · [Polish access](https://www.gdziepolek.pl/produkty/71824/skinoren-zel/apteki) · [Azelaic Acid trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11116308/)

The azelaic trial had 60 completers and tested a different manufacturer's 15% gel; 20/30 treated participants initially reported mild irritation. Other acne treatments were excluded, so it does not validate adding Skinoren to this Isotretinoin regimen. Its Polish label advises reducing use or pausing for irritation and reviewing acne treatment if no improvement after a month. Likewise, photoaging trials using frequent retinoid application do not establish equal effects from once-weekly use.

**Coordinate actives with E.1.1 Isotretinoin.** Its label discourages concurrent topical keratolytic/exfoliating acne products because of irritation. Separating nights does not settle compatibility. The proposal retires the scheduled AHA 30%/BHA 2% peel and keeps current oral/topical prescription doses unchanged while the prescriber reviews the plan. Keep actives off irritated eyelids; persistent dryness/redness warrants assessment before resurfacing. [Izotek label](https://rejestrymedyczne.ezdrowie.gov.pl/api/rpl/medicinal-products/16306/characteristic)

## Face and eye procedures

### A better pore-treatment decision

| Option | Where it fits | Evidence and practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ResurFX 1565nm** | Retained nonablative fractional option for a defined pore/texture course. | Expect a series and reassessment rather than assuming one annual visit is sufficient. Response, downtime and pigmentation risk depend on settings and skin type. |
| **Fractional 1064nm picosecond laser** | New alternative worth comparing when recovery matters. | A 20-person fixed-side split-face study, 19 completers, used five monthly treatments. Both sides improved; final pore-count difference was not significant. Pico produced less pain and hyperpigmentation in that cohort. This is promising small-study evidence, not proof of universal superiority. |
| **Fractional CO2** | Retained option when texture/scarring and accepted recovery justify it. | A small pore trial found improvement with both CO2 and its Nd:YAG comparator; it does not establish CO2 as the mandatory next step. |

[Direct pico/ResurFX comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36718089/) · [CO2 pore trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34255869/)

Ask for the **exact device and fractional handpiece**, expected downtime and a treatment course tailored to the examination. PicoWay Resolve 1064 is documented by a [Polish distributor](https://novagroup.pl/produkt/picoway/), but the exact handpiece at a local clinic remains unverified. A generic pico/tattoo-removal listing is insufficient. Select one primary course and coordinate any microneedling on the same area. Review matched photographs about 3 months after the final pore-treatment session before deciding maintenance. This timing is an assessment plan, not a requirement to buy five sessions.

### Match eye-area care to the actual problem

- **Dryness:** keep Dermallergo Eyes as the lighter option and Atoderm Intensive Eye for richer coverage; establish comfortable skin before adding procedures. Creams cannot be assumed to correct structural hollowing.
- **Persistent crepey texture/fine lines:** Tixel remains an option. A 68-person randomized study found similar moderate improvement to 1565nm laser; Tixel hurt less but caused more crusting beyond 48h. It is one coordinated face/eyelid course, not two separate bookings. [Tixel trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34787919/)
- **PRF:** a bounded optional trial for a defined mild skin-quality/dark-circle concern. A 2026 split-face PRP/PRF trial with 24 completers found improvements but generally no clear between-treatment advantage; severe hollowing was inadequately corrected. It does not establish twice-yearly maintenance. [Trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13284528/)
- **INFINI Premium VE:** the manufacturer identifies fragmented Hyaluronic Acid plus amino acids. No independent comparative finished-product trial was verified. Keep it conditional on an exact-product/indication discussion. It is not the Infini RF device or a polynucleotide preparation. [Manufacturer](https://infinifiller.com/ve-infini-premium-filler/)
- **Polynucleotides:** potentially interesting, but not prioritized here. The small 27-person PN-versus-HA trial did not show a clear visible-aesthetic score advantage. A larger often-cited comparison was retracted in March 2026; exclude it from efficacy reasoning. [Small trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32248707/) · [Retraction](https://doi.org/10.1111/jocd.70794)

[Sculptra instructions](https://www.sculptrausa.com/sites/default/files/2023-09/Sculptra-e-IFU-USA.pdf) · [Radiesse instructions](https://radiesse.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/instructions-for-use-radiesse-wrinkles.pdf)

Confirm the current device IFU and prescriber plan, particularly for Tixel, rather than inventing a universal waiting interval or stopping E.1.1 automatically. [Consensus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28498204/) · [Fractional CO2 trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37555247/) · [Tixel trial eligibility](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04730336)

## Hair, brows and beard

**Keep E.1.2 Dutasteride 0.5mg e2d and E.2 oral Minoxidil 5mg unchanged.** The concrete next step is one prescriber-documented plan covering scalp, brows and beard: indication, application area/volume, whether each topical is an adjunct or a replacement, and tolerability/BP/pulse review. Use standardized photos and, where available, trichoscopy at baseline and 4–6 months. A trial comparing oral 5mg with topical 5% in 90 men did not test the combination; it cannot prove that an add-on has no benefit. [Oral-versus-topical trial](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamadermatology/fullarticle/2817326)

The proposed scalp **Minoxidil 7% + Dutasteride 0.25% + Caffeine 0.2%** and brow **Bimatoprost 0.03% + Minoxidil 5% + Cetirizine 1%** formulations remain pending pharmacist/prescriber review, not confirmed active treatments. Verify ingredients, vehicle stability, application volume, beyond-use date and the replacement/add-on decision before use. No alternative compounded formula is silently substituted in this PR.

Topical dutasteride has promising human data: a 24-week phase II trial in 135 men tested 0.01–0.05% in a specific vehicle, with industry involvement. It compared against oral finasteride, not oral dutasteride, and was not an add-on to this stack. Its maximum tested concentration does not validate 0.25% or the proposed multi-ingredient mixture. A separate 5%-versus-10% minoxidil trial also cautions against assuming a simple concentration-response benefit; it did not test 7%. [Topical dutasteride trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12405733/) · [Minoxidil concentration trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31403367/)

**Bimatoprost has real eyebrow evidence.** A 357-person trial supports 0.03% once-daily use for eyebrow hypotrichosis, but not the proposed triple mixture. Keep it off the eyes/eyelids and reassess new hollowing, eyelid changes or pigmentation. EMA describes such changes during ophthalmic use; their frequency with carefully limited eyebrow application is uncertain. Beard minoxidil also has small controlled studies, but their dosing/populations do not establish the exact current multi-site combination. [Brow trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5414776/) · [Lumigan information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/lumigan-epar-product-information_en.pdf) · [Beard trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26893270/)

Keep Philip Kingsley shampoo/conditioner and established barber/brow styling. Nizoral is retained for weekly maintenance/prevention of recurrent dandruff/seborrhoeic dermatitis; active flares have a different labeled schedule. Its presence is not proof of extra regrowth over the oral core. [Polish Nizoral label](https://rejestrymedyczne.ezdrowie.gov.pl/api/rpl/medicinal-products/4780/characteristic) Microblading becomes an option for residual gaps/faded pigment after growth is assessed. Hair styling products remain the separate scope of [PR #6](https://github.com/PawelTroka/health-protocol/pull/6).

### Adjuncts and retired defaults

- **Scalp needling:** positive trials justify an optional clinician-defined scalp course, with an explicit topical restart plan. They do not validate brow/beard dermastamping or applying RU/minoxidil/bimatoprost through freshly injured skin. The minoxidil label specifies intact scalp. [Scalp trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3746236/) · [Alocutan label](https://rejestry.ezdrowie.gov.pl/api/rpl/medicinal-products/35273/characteristic)
- **PRP:** retain as optional where previous photos/trichoscopy demonstrate benefit. Trials vary, including a 35-person split-scalp study without a significant advantage over saline. Do not automatically restart an induction or continue indefinitely by calendar. [Induction trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30141776/) · [Placebo-controlled trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32653577/)
- **RU58841:** the 2026 human study detected parent drug/metabolite in blood after a single scalp 30mg dose in six men. It establishes systemic exposure, not chronic clinical safety, harm or hair efficacy. Routine twice-daily use leaves the active plan; preserve the reason in Removed items. [Human study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13487499/)
- **Unspecified annual exosomes:** remain outside routine maintenance until the exact product, route, sterility/quality and credible matched clinical evidence are known. FDA warnings are US regulatory context, not a determination of Polish legality for a particular product. [FDA notification](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/public-safety-notification-exosome-products)

### Keep light treatment proportional to its evidence

Facial photobiomodulation and scalp low-level light therapy have controlled human studies, so keeping an existing tolerated device is reasonable. Results are device/protocol-specific: one 2025 facial trial found no between-group difference on the main wrinkle-rating scale despite some photographic/satisfaction improvements. Scalp sham-controlled trials support possible density gains over several months. [Facial trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40167796/) · [Scalp trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23551662/)

The exact current Ziree mask/cap instructions and clinical equivalence were not verified; the linked pages were inaccessible during this review. Confirm model, wavelength, delivered dose/session time and eye-safety instructions from the device documentation. 807/272 emitters alone are not an effective-dose specification. Keep existing frequency pending that check; no new device purchase or universal session duration is prescribed.

RiseWell Original Mineral uses 10% micro-hydroxyapatite; the nano-containing PRO product is different. Clean between teeth daily with fitted brushes where they fit and floss/SuperFloss for tighter contacts or retainers. Keep Waterpik as an adjunct. Brush 2min twice daily and spit without immediately rinsing. The suggested separate rinse timing follows general fluoride-retention guidance; OraLactin's own brochure specifies after brushing. Preserve its twice-daily selection and agree sequencing with the dentist. [EFP interdental guidance](https://www.efp.org/for-patients/gum-diseases/gum-disease-prevention/) · [NHS fluoride guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/healthy-teeth-and-gums/how-to-keep-your-teeth-clean/) · [ApaCare brochure](https://www.apacare.com/media/0f/a5/90/1741777448/AC_OL_Imagebroschre_03_2025%20engl_web.pdf?ts=1741777448)

**Professional care becomes individualized.** A 1,877-person randomized study of regular dental attenders found no clinically important bleeding advantage from automatic 6-monthly versus 12-monthly/no scheduled scale-and-polish. It does not rule out frequent care for periodontal treatment, braces or cosmetic stain removal. Examination and cleaning intervals are different decisions; synchronize them with the dentist rather than carrying conflicting monthly/every 2 months instructions. [IQuaD trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29984691/)

For a visible smile improvement, use **assessment → targeted stain polishing → shade-planned natural-tooth whitening if needed → selected white-spot treatment/bonding if still indicated**. Composite does not lighten like natural teeth; agree the final shade and any repair/replacement plan before whitening. Resin infiltration can help appropriate post-orthodontic white spots, supported by a 79-person trial; it is not suitable for every white lesion. Existing retainers and bite-led orthodontic/maxillofacial decisions remain. [ADA whitening](https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/whitening) · [White-spot trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37603170/)

**Mouthwash reconciliation stays separate.** This PR preserves the recently selected OraLactin, Eludril and Curasept block. Its automatic post-cleaning Chlorhexidine instruction still merits a dentist-directed indication/duration correction, to keep with the oral-shopping reconciliation associated with [PR #40](https://github.com/PawelTroka/health-protocol/pull/40). Chlorhexidine is treatment for a specific need rather than an automatic response to every professional clean; EFP highlights time limits and staining. [EFP guidance](https://www.efp.org/for-patients/gum-diseases/gum-disease-prevention/)

## Body contour and skin

The [surgery record](../README.md#11-surgeries) documents a no-mesh epigastric hernia repair and a May 2026 ultrasound without recurrence, with separate linea-alba widening. No active hernia is inferred. An examination should distinguish residual localized fat, muscle definition, skin laxity and abdominal-wall structure before another contouring course.

**EmSculpt Neo stays optional.** A 72-person sham-controlled trial found ultrasound changes in fat/muscle thickness after a course. It does not establish equal gains in a lean, trained man, correction of linea-alba widening, or an annual-repeat requirement. Use prior visible response, consistent photographs and abdominal-wall/device assessment to decide whether further sessions are worthwhile. [Trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35259147/) · [Device/body-contouring guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/non-invasive-body-contouring-technologies)

**Aqualyx's preset 4-vial/month/annual schedule is retired.** Any treatment needs an exact-product, current local indication/IFU and in-person abdominal-wall assessment. The current EU certificate/IFU was not verified. FDA warnings about Aqualyx concern US approval and reported injection harms; they do not establish Polish illegality. VASER remains a surgical consultation option for a persistent, appropriate target after stable weight; its brand alone does not establish superior results. [Injection warning](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/buying-using-medicine-safely/using-fat-dissolving-injections-are-not-fda-approved-can-be-harmful)

Keep the existing body/hand cleansers and emollients. **Replenishment identity needs attention:** the linked Lipikar AP+M bundle/official Polish page now point toward AP+MAX, an updated formula. Do not silently treat them as identical; the personal-care shopping audit owns the replacement decision. [Current manufacturer formula](https://www.laroche-posay.pl/lipikar/lipikar-ap-plus-max-baume)
