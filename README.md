## 1. Sleep

**Protect sleep first; plan work, meals and training around it.**

### Schedule & wind-down

- **Sleep:** ~8h asleep; reserve ~9h in bed. Keep bed/wake times within 30–60min, including weekends.

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| After waking | **20–30min outdoors**; daylight during the day with [UV protection](#skincare). |
| ≥8–10h before bed | Stop caffeine/stimulants; earlier after high doses or if sensitive. Count [Matcha and EvoBomb's 200mg Caffeine](#timing--training). |
| **3h before bed** | **Dim red lights**; dim screens and avoid bright overhead lighting. |
| 2–3h before bed | Finish large meals; a light snack is fine. Avoid alcohol. |
| Final 30–60min | Quiet reading or relaxation; stop stimulating work and bright screens. |
| **00:00–10:14** | Devices off or **Do Not Disturb**; start earlier if bedtime is earlier. |

- **Optional wind-down:** finish Sauna 1–3h before bed; sex in the final hour, if relaxing.
- **[Exercise](#3-exercise):** move intense evening sessions earlier if they disrupt sleep.

### Bedroom setup

| Setting | Personal target |
|---|---|
| 🌡️ Temperature | **18°C**; adjust [bedding](#bedding-and-textiles) for comfort. |
| 💧 Humidity | **50% RH**; lower if condensation appears. |
| 🌑 Light | **Blackout** blinds, covered LEDs; lights off for sleep. |
| 🔇 Noise | **<30dBA** overnight; quiet fans. |
| 🛏️ Bed | Sleep and intimacy only. |

### Air quality

| Measure | Overnight target | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **CO₂** | **<800ppm** | Increase fresh-air ventilation if repeatedly **≥1,000ppm**. |
| **PM2.5** | **<10µg/m³**; lower is better | Run **HEPA filtration all night**; increase speed if readings stay high. |

- **Monitor:** log CO₂, PM2.5, temperature and RH with [air-quality monitors](#clean-air). Use NDIR for CO₂; place at head height, >50cm from sleepers, away from windows/vents.
- **Size:** **CADR (m³/h) ≥5 × bedroom volume (m³)** at the **actual overnight fan speed**.
- **Ventilate:** check [outdoor air](https://www.iqair.com/air-quality-map/poland/pomerania/gdansk); use filtered fresh air when polluted. HEPA and ordinary split AC do not remove CO₂.
- **Upkeep:** no smoke, scented candles, incense or ozone generators. Replace filters per manual; empty/dry/refill humidifier tanks daily and clean per manual. Fix dampness/mold at its source.

### Tracking & troubleshooting

- **Review:** [Oura + daytime alertness](#daily-tracking), **2–4-week trends**; adjust sleep opportunity to need, not wearable sleep-stage scores.
- **Wide awake/frustrated in bed:** get up; read quietly in dim light; return when sleepy.
- **Get assessed:** persistent insomnia, loud snoring, gasping or unexplained daytime sleepiness. [CBT-I](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/insomnia/treatment) is first-line for chronic insomnia.

## 3. Exercise

### Ultimate Optimized Daily Activity & Training Routine: Aesthetics, Longevity & Sexual Health

#### Core Philosophy:
- **Looksmaxxing (Aesthetics):** Focuses heavily on the V-taper (broad shoulders/lats), an upper chest "shelf", a masculine neck, and thick forearms. **Zero direct oblique work** and stomach vacuums are included to keep the waist tight. Direct quad work is minimized to prevent a "blocky" lower half, focusing instead on longevity, mobility and an athletic posterior chain.
- **Longevity & Health:** Ego lifting is removed. Use a **3-0-1-0 tempo** (3 seconds lowering, 1 second lifting) to protect joints and maximize hypertrophy. **Hernia & Diastasis Recti Care:** Exhale on the concentric (lifting) phase—no Valsalva maneuver—to manage intra-abdominal pressure. **The No-Doming/No-Coning Rule:** Focus on deep Transverse Abdominis (TVA) drawing (belly button to spine) and prevent any stomach doming/coning (midline ridge) during core work. Spinal loading is minimized by prioritizing chest-supported back exercises and dumbbell variations over heavy barbell lifts.
- **Sexual Health:** Heavy emphasis on glutes, hamstrings, and hip hinge movements (like Hip Thrusts) for hip power and pelvic floor health. Mandatory Zone 2 cardio is included for cardiovascular and endothelial health.
- **Synergy:** Workouts are structured to minimize overlap. Push days alternate between (Chest Press + Lateral Raise) and (Shoulder Press + Pec Fly). Pull days combine lat work, chest-supported rows and brief scapular-control drills. Gym workouts are compressed into **5 consecutive weekdays**, leaving your weekends completely free of typical gym lifts to focus on outdoor conditioning and socializing. Four weekday workouts finish with 20 minutes of easy Zone 2 cardio; the timing and modalities are selected to limit interference with lifting while supporting cardiovascular health.
- **Personal Structural Assessment:** Fairly aesthetic frame overall. My proportions are actually closer to athletic aesthetic build, "men's physique" type frame, than to ultra-wide comic-book "classic physique" clavicle genetics.
  - **Measurements:** [Body and facial measurements](results.md#body-measurements), latest reported 2026-11-07.
  - **Neck:** slightly on the **longer side**. It gives a more "model/athletic" silhouette rather than compact powerlifter proportions. Traps are not overpowering the neck, which accentuates this further.
  - **Clavicles:** **medium** length, maybe *slightly short relative to muscularity*, but not genuinely "short clavicles." Shoulders don't look narrow. The issue is more that arm/chest mass dominates the frame, which can visually compress shoulder width. Getting leaner makes the structure actually looks broader.

### 🍳 Preparation & food safety

- **Batch simply:** choose a few Vegetable rotations per shopping trip. Steam the marked Vegetables, Potatoes/Sweet Potatoes and Mushrooms until appropriately tender; follow the separate Quinoa, Pulse and food-safety instructions. Cool cooked leftovers promptly, refrigerate and reheat safely.

Drain, rinse and weigh the cooked serving.
- **Cook safely:** Poultry 74°C and Fish 63°C at the core, or a validated time–temperature equivalent. Cook Eggs, Shellfish and Mushrooms thoroughly; oven temperature alone does not establish safety. [Safe temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) Keep the skinless Salmon at 0–2°C and follow its delivered use-by date; it is sold thawed, so do not refreeze.

Watercress Leaves/Microgreens and Arugula need no routine cooking. [Produce preparation](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-produce-safely) For Spinach/Chard, boiling and discarding the Water remains an optional preparation to reduce oxalates. [Oxalate preparation study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15826055/)
- **Sprouts:** rinse Broccoli/Radish Sprouts and steam until steaming hot throughout. Washing or light warming does not reliably remove their bacterial risk. [Sprout safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-produce-safely)

## 5. Supplements and drugs
Supplements and drugs are very well focused in this protocol because of how easy it is to "just take a pill". Granted, usually mastering the basics (sleep, diet, exercise) does bring you more health benefits but supplements help even if you don't.
Within each timing, entries are ordered roughly by evidence-weighted expected net benefit; multi-ingredient products can contain components with different evidence ranks. Active entries generally use generic ingredient names, while exact manufacturer, product, branded-formulation or strain names remain where identity or provenance materially defines the selected intervention; the links identify the exact selected products, and the Notes explain deeper formulation evidence. Numeric quantities use compact number-unit formatting, such as `1g`, `250mg` and `1scoop`. List order does not create additional timing windows. Evidence medals precede supplement codes: 🥇 Gold marks the strongest relevant human evidence plus meaningful expected benefit, 🥈 Silver marks supportive but smaller, indirect or limited human evidence and/or a more modest marginal benefit, and 🥉 Bronze marks an exploratory intervention supported mainly by mechanisms, animal work, pharmacokinetics or small pilots without an established outcome benefit. A medal grades the exact active intervention in this protocol—not product purity, safety, organizer order or a guaranteed individual response; Bronze is not automatically a removal recommendation, and an indication, biomarker or reproducible response can change a tier. Drugs, Water and EVOO remain intentionally unmedalled, and blend medals apply to the overall intervention rather than every nested ingredient.
The Morning, Lunch and Evening organizers each use two linked 9-compartment [GymBeam Adjustable PillBoxes](https://gymbeam.com/adjustable-pillbox-gymbeam.html), while Before Workout and After Workout each use one. Timing codes map the protocol directly to preparation: `M`, `L`, `B`, `IW`, `A` and `E` mean Morning, Lunch, Before Workout, Intra-Workout, After Workout and Evening; numbered pill codes are physical organizer compartments; `.1` and `.2` divide one compartment between two small, clearly distinguishable pills; a range such as `L.2-3` means one large pill in each listed compartment; and a multiplier within one code, such as `M.15 3x`, means the small pills share that compartment. Zero-series codes such as `M.0.2` identify drinks or powders outside the pill organizer and do not consume a compartment. In a deeper code such as `M.0.3.2.1`, the next number identifies the bold ingredient subgroup and the final number identifies that ingredient's position within the subgroup. Slot-code superscripts on nested ingredients point to the corresponding Notes topic and never consume organizer compartments. Empty compartments remain strategically positioned at the largest practical evidence-benefit gaps.
