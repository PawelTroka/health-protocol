# Personal-care and household shopping: subscription audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08.** Scope: the consumable products in the canonical [Looksmaxxing](../README.md#6-looksmaxxing), [Environment](../README.md#8-environment), hygiene, laundry and dishwasher sections. The food, swallowed supplements and nutritional powders are separate shopping workstreams. Alternative products in an `or` list remain alternatives; this is not a basket containing all of them.

## Buying decision

**Keep the verified manual routes below. No subscription in this category has yet passed all of the protocol's requirements: the correct formulation, actual automatic renewal, Polish home delivery without compulsory delivery fees, and no more than a 25% delivered-price premium.** This is a finding about the routes checked, not a claim that subscriptions cannot exist elsewhere.

There are useful genuine subscription leads, especially Liki24, but its cosmetic prices frequently exceed the cap before delivery. Its 1 l CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser is the closest documented candidate; its final eligibility depends on the current manual comparator, a genuinely needed basket and the bulky-item fee. The audit preserves that working product link instead of labeling the whole skincare routine automated.

Two immediate improvements are concrete: buy the exact Denkmit Ultra Sensitive colour powder directly from dm at 18.95 PLN/1.35 kg, and avoid treating Bioshi's current `Regularnie` purchase reminders as unattended replenishment. The current white/light dm pack has a different GTIN from the original offer and remains a candidate pending formulation comparison. Current product and subscription terms supersede old indexed discounts.

### Quality and comparison rules

- Preserve the selected finished formula, concentration and intended use. A tinted sunscreen, a face cream with SPF, a foaming cleanser, a replacement detergent formulation or a differently sized bottle is not automatically interchangeable with the selected SKU.
- Prefer manufacturer or established pharmacy/drogerie supply and the exact labeled product. Merchant claims such as “natural,” “non-toxic,” “anti-aging,” or a sustainability score do not establish better longevity or sexual-health outcomes.
- Prices below are public VAT-inclusive observations in PLN unless a currency is explicit. They exclude delivery unless stated. `Listed` means a price was exposed without a reliable product-level stock control; `available` means the page displayed availability or an orderable offer. No unobserved checkout total is called verified.
- A “cheapest” claim is limited to comparable offers actually verified here. Marketplace default sellers, prices and stock change; older search snippets and an archived offer's price are not current purchasable comparators. Unknown is left unknown.
- Flag a premium of at least 10%, or at least 10 PLN per 30 days when consumption supports that calculation. The hard selection cap remains 25% **including all compulsory fees**. Savings against a merchant's crossed-out list price are not savings against Polish manual shopping.
- Do not infer consumption in ml/day from “twice daily.” Only the dishwasher tablet and maintenance-cleaner instructions specify quantities per use. No invented monthly skincare cost or automatic cadence appears below.
