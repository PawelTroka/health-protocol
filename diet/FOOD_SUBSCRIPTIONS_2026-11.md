## Purchasing decision

**Keep the verified manual routes and treat Brown House & Tea matcha as a conditional subscription candidate. No complete automatic food basket was verified to meet all the existing constraints.** There are useful genuine automatic programmes, but a badge alone does not establish the exact product, Polish home delivery, a tolerable premium and a sustainable quantity.

The material change is at **Bioshi**: its [current terms, §5](https://bioshi.pl/pl/terms) say that since **23 October 2026** the old programme no longer automatically places orders or charges saved cards. `Regularnie` sends a checkout proposal; the customer must confirm and pay. The first qualifying order has10% off and later confirmed orders15%, but this is **manual confirmation**, not unattended replenishment. Historical account records remain evidence of what happened at their dates; they do not establish current automatic coverage.

This audit repairs the withdrawn organic arugula link and inaccessible sardine offer using verified current equivalents, and removes stale automatic-food markers. It does not activate, cancel, pay for or modify any merchant account plan.

## What “best” means here

1. **Preserve the food actually selected:** organic where specified; raw unsalted nuts where specified; whole seeds for freshly grinding; Ceylon rather than generic cinnamon; lactose-free and fortification requirements; exact fish species, drained mass and cold-chain form; meaningful identities such as Pamako.
2. **Compare the amount actually consumed:** net or drained edible mass, not misleading gross pack sizes; do not treat dried and cooked pulse weights as equivalent. A lower unit price can still waste money when a pack spoils or accumulates.
3. **Require native automatic ordering and payment.** Saved baskets, stock alerts, discount memberships and emails requiring checkout do not meet the task.
4. **Apply the existing free-home-courier rule and maximum25% delivered-price premium per comparable product.** Lockers, Smart membership conditions and introductory coupons are not unconditional free home delivery.
5. **Flag meaningful premiums at10% or10PLN/30days**, even below the25% ceiling. Product-price comparisons are separated from delivery because a postcode, basket and actual recurring checkout were not authorized or supplied.
6. **Do not infer better health, longevity, appearance or sexual function from price, “premium,” organic certification or a retailer’s antioxidant copy.** Quality documentation is useful for identity and contamination control; it does not establish a superior clinical outcome. No replacement weakens a named food attribute merely to obtain autoship.

Prices are public observations from8Nov2026, inPLN including consumer VAT where displayed, excluding delivery unless expressly stated. “Lowest checked” means among verified comparable offers in this audit, not a guarantee across every Polish shop. Availability and a regional fresh-food slot can differ by delivery address. A merchant’s current product page takes precedence over an older indexed snippet. Unknowns are deliberately left unknown.

### Conditional matcha route

The [Brown organic Japanese matcha40g](https://brownhouseandtea.pl/produkt/matcha-zielona-herbata-matcha-bio) is54PLN one-time. The published8-week15% plan implies **45.90PLN/40g**. Three packs would be **137.70PLN for120g every56days**, above the public119PLN free-home threshold if subscriptions preserve that threshold. The100g refill was unavailable.

At the unchanged2g/day,56days consume112g: this plan adds **8g stock per cycle**, roughly52g/year. It is a workable candidate to review, but not indefinitely balanced unattended replenishment. One pack/4weeks supplies40g against56g used and also misses free shipping; three packs/4weeks oversupply markedly. Do not quietly rely on routine manual skips to claim the plan meets the existing constraints.

The comparable normal [Moya Codzienna100g](https://gemini.pl/moya-matcha-codzienna-organiczna-japonska-herbata-100-g-0112317) quote is101.99PLN. Brown’s normalized114.75/100g is **12.5% more**, approximately **7.66PLN per30days** at60g consumption, assuming free delivery on both consolidated baskets. A separate Gemini short-date category listing showed71.29/100g with10Feb2027 expiry; against that temporary, not checkout-verified clearance, the premium is61.0%. That cannot be treated as a permanent manual price.

Other leads were weaker: [M80](https://m80matcha.pl/produkt/matcha-ni/) advertises real monthly10% auto savings but organic certification and exact-price/size matching were unresolved; [Linea Secco Nocellara500ml](https://lineasecco.com/sklep/oliwa-nocellara-etnea-500ml) offers a10% oil subscription but was on preorder and did not verify the specified organic Pamako/Phileos identity, lot analysis or free home terms. Neither replaces the active products.

## Practical decisions from this audit

- **Immediate link repairs:** use current organic Fit & Easy arugula80g at Frisco and exact Delicius sardines120g/85g drained at Frisco. Preserve food names and portions.
