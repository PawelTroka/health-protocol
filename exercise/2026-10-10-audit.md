# Exercise optimization audit — 2026-10-10

Baseline: main at 0ff39af9. Only section 3 of README is modified. The October 5 pec-deck and row-load updates were already implemented; October 8 changed only SLDL and shoulder-press reference weights. The current exercise section remains the sole active prescription.

## Decisions and evidence
- **Lateral delts:** Thursday raises are no longer optional, replacing the pullover rather than adding workload. Cable and dumbbell raises produced similar hypertrophy in an eight-week comparison; no special stretched-position superiority is claimed. [2025 study](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2025.1611468). Main benefit is consistency with the user's shoulder-width priority.
- **Hamstrings:** Seated leg curls replace kickbacks to provide knee flexion in a hinge-dominant day. Seated curls outperformed prone curls for hamstring growth in one 12-week within-person MRI study; this does not directly establish superiority to kickbacks for glutes. [Maeo et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33009197/). Expect local soreness; use tolerable knee range.
- **Hinge:** Dumbbell RDL replaces SLDL with soft knees and reproducible hip range. This is a coaching/tolerance choice, *not* proof of lower spinal load or greater hypertrophy. Redundant rounded-back extensions are removed to reduce fatigue.
- **Back:** Neutral pulldown replaces the default weighted pull-up for finer load/range adjustment in the setting of recurrent shoulder symptoms. Friday's row becomes chest-supported to reduce unsupported trunk demands. Neither swap guarantees superior lat growth or zero shoulder/spinal stress. Re-baseline weights.
- **Scapular work:** Wall slides and light prone Y raises replace one forearm curl and the Kelso shrug slot. A shoulder-pain RCT supports targeted scapular training as a possible rehabilitation component, but cannot diagnose this user's pain or establish these exact drills as universally superior. [Clinical trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38376127/). Avoid painful arcs; persistent clicking/pain merits assessment. Reverse pec deck, face pulls and one direct reverse-curl slot remain.
- **Core:** Hanging raises and duplicate crunch slots are reduced to controlled floor-based and cable movements. This avoids repeated overhead suspension, but no particular exercise is proven to close male rectus diastasis or prevent hernia recurrence. [European Hernia Society guidance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34595502/). Monitor symptoms and new bulging.
- **Effort and tempo:** 1-3 RIR and repeatable progression are favored over automatic load increases or obligatory failure. Similar hypertrophy with lower fatigue has been observed at 1-2 RIR versus failure in trained participants. [Trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38393985/). No unique injury-prevention claim for 3-0-1-0 tempo.
- **Aerobic:** Four 20min weekday sessions can progress toward 25-30min; Saturday 60min is retained. Nominal easy aerobic volume becomes 140→160→180min/week, excluding other activities. These are targets, not proof that the previous total was inadequate. [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity). Sunday intervals become a controlled option, not mandatory maximal sprinting. [Intervals RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17414804/).
- **Retained:** Monday pec deck, incline/reverse-grip press alternative, reverse pec deck, triceps overhead extension, split squats, calf work, chest-supported Tuesday row, arm training and outdoor sport. The pullover is not uniquely required for an upper-chest shelf. Novel BFR, flywheel, forced lengthened partials, Nordics and mandatory supersets are deferred: their marginal benefit does not clearly exceed complexity, soreness or symptom cost here.

## Reassessment
Over 6-8 weeks record load, reps, estimated RIR, shoulder and abdominal symptoms during and next morning, duration and aerobic effort. Compare standardized photos and measurements. Keep substitutions only if tolerability, execution or performance per unit time improves. Persistent painful shoulder clicking, weakness, night pain, or a new abdominal bulge warrants clinical assessment rather than further exercise experimentation.

This is individualized training guidance, not a diagnosis or a guarantee of posture correction, injury prevention, longevity or improved sexual function. No diet, supplements or other unrelated sections are changed.
