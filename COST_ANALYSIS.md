# Monthly cost of the full health protocol

**Price basis:** general model 2026-08-18; Phileos cooking EVOO checked 2026-08-22  
**Location and currency:** Gdańsk / Poland, PLN  
**Protocol quantities:** [README.md](README.md), updated through 2026-08-22  
**Purpose:** budgeting only; this is not a recommendation that every item or procedure is medically appropriate.

## Executive result

The most useful planning estimate for the protocol is **12,282 PLN/month** (**147,379 PLN/year**) in mature steady state. This includes a monthly reserve for annual and multi-year testing, procedures, vaccinations, and replacement of equipment.

| Budget view | Monthly | Annual | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Recurring cash-like spend | **11,827 PLN** | **141,927 PLN** | Food, consumables, subscriptions, services, and reserves for infrequent tests/procedures; excludes device depreciation |
| Steady-state economic cost | **12,282 PLN** | **147,379 PLN** | Recurring cash-like spend plus **454 PLN/month** device/cookware replacement reserve |
| First-year effective cost | **13,061–13,228 PLN** | **156,735–158,735 PLN** | Steady state plus the extra 2026 Sculptra/PRF sessions and the finite Aqualyx and 4–6-session EmSculpt Neo phases |
| Plausible steady-state range | **10,800–13,900 PLN** | **129,600–166,800 PLN** | Main uncertainty is unspecified food additions, facilities, product usage, provider quotes, and unavailable listings |

If none of the capital equipment is already owned, the listed setup costs are about **20,264 PLN paid up front**, excluding Qalzy (no public scale price), installation, delivery, import VAT/duty, and optional cookware.

The full literal model includes products that their sellers label **research-use-only and not intended for human use**. Removing the six injectable research compounds and the RU58841 topical assumption lowers the estimate by about **1,558 PLN/month**, to approximately **10,724 PLN/month** including device amortization.

## 10. Exchange rates, rules and exclusions

Foreign prices use the [NBP 2026-08-18 table](https://api.nbp.pl/api/exchangerates/tables/a/2026-08-18/?format=json):

- EUR/PLN **4.3189**
- USD/PLN **3.7307**
- GBP/PLN **5.0487**

Calculation rules:

- month = 365.25 / 12 = **30.4375 days**
- week = 365.25 / 12 / 7 = **4.3482 weeks/month**
- five workout days/week = **21.741 workout days/month**
- infrequent items are divided by their interval: six-month cost / 6, annual / 12, five-year / 60
- current sale/subscription prices are used when they are the normal purchasable route; crossed-out “was” prices are not used
- no shipping, import tax, appointment travel, consultation required only to obtain prescriptions, or financing cost
- historical surgeries are excluded; “as needed,” conditional and optional items are excluded unless a frequency is explicit
- the result is a budget model, not a clinical assessment of benefit, duplication, safety, or appropriate test frequency
