## Morning evidence review

### Nutrient coverage in this protocol

Nutritional adequacy has substantial value, particularly in a calorie-restricted diet. Individual additional ingredients can nevertheless have modest value when foods and other products already contribute the same nutrient. The [NIH multivitamin assessment](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/MVMS-HealthProfessional/) supports filling intake gaps while distinguishing this from reliable chronic-disease prevention. The grades below recognize practical coverage without treating every essential nutrient as automatically Gold. The [protocol's nutrient totals and follow-up](../README.md#totals) supply the current context: existing high B6/B12 or Zinc results constrain the expected benefit of further supply; ordinary on-treatment results do **not** establish that the current regimen is unnecessary or that a particular ingredient caused the result.
