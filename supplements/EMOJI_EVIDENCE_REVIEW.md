## Morning evidence review

### Nutrient coverage in this protocol

Nutritional adequacy has substantial value, particularly in a calorie-restricted diet. Individual additional ingredients can nevertheless have modest value when foods and other products already contribute the same nutrient. The [NIH multivitamin assessment](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/MVMS-HealthProfessional/) supports filling intake gaps while distinguishing this from reliable chronic-disease prevention. The grades below recognize practical coverage without treating every essential nutrient as automatically Gold. The [protocol's nutrient totals and follow-up](../README.md#totals) supply the current context: existing high B6/B12 or Zinc results constrain the expected benefit of further supply; ordinary on-treatment results do **not** establish that the current regimen is unnecessary or that a particular ingredient caused the result.

## Food medal coverage

The food review considers the written portions, complete meals, training and body-composition goals, GI tolerance and existing intake. Alternatives replace the relevant food-group choice; close cultivars and brands are not presented as precisely ranked clinical comparisons.

The main food pattern has high combined value because it supplies adequate protein and energy, fiber, varied plants and unsaturated fats consistently. The [WHO healthy-diet guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet) supports these foundations. **Low-dose tirzepatide evidence.** A [2026 prospective cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42521631/) followed 112 non-diabetic Japanese adults with obesity: 58 remained on 2.5mg and 54 used 5mg, with mean six-month losses of 15.3% and 16.1%. Conversely, the [26-week 1mg randomized diabetes trial](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/trial/2016-004179-33/results) found −0.9kg versus −0.4kg with placebo, an uncertain −0.5kg difference (95% CI −2.7 to 1.7). Together these findings support low-dose plausibility while leaving the magnitude of benefit at 1.5mg unresolved.
