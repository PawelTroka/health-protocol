## Decision rules

| Medal | Meaning | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🥇 Gold | Established support for the main stated benefit, through consistent human evidence or a well-established physiological effect at a relevant dose/form | A large effect, longer human life, an exact-brand trial, a need to add the intervention or a guaranteed individual response |
| 🥈 Silver | Promising human evidence with meaningful limits in replication, consistency or applicability | Ineffectiveness, an endorsement of every secondary target or proven superiority over the current product |
| 🥉 Bronze | Exploratory, mixed, unsupported or negative findings for the proposed use; mainly mechanistic evidence; or a substantial exposure/application mismatch | A recommendation to experiment, proof of safety or certainty that the intervention can never work |

1. **Grade the stated purpose.** Nutrient-provision medals grade contribution to established requirements and normal physiological roles. A targeted intervention is graded for its clinical or functional outcome, dose and population. The row's location does not change the rule. Magnesium provision can be Gold while its sleep-treatment claim is unestablished; iron can be an essential nutrient while deliberately raising stores without deficiency remains an uncertain use.
2. **Match meaningful exposure.** Consider the active ingredient, amount, daily total, formulation, route and conditional schedule. Evidence can transfer between materially equivalent products without a trial of every retail brand. Probiotic strain, extract markers, release system, fresh-root equivalence and materially different doses still matter.
3. **Keep evidence separate from effect size and personal value.** A modest replicated symptom, functional or physiological effect can earn Gold. A supported marker change is graded as that marker change, without inventing event prevention. NAD, hormone or aging-clock changes alone do not establish clinical benefit. Record positive newer findings alongside null primary outcomes.
4. **Grade a blend's main supported purpose.** Effective principal ingredients can justify Gold without a finished-blend trial. This does not prove synergy, every constituent's benefit or an extra benefit from stacking. Separately assessed ingredients carry their own medals; composition-only amounts are disclosures, not new efficacy claims.
5. **Read target icons in context.** Food icons can describe nutrition, care icons a cosmetic target, and test icons the system measured. They are not diagnoses. A single medal grades the primary stated claim; secondary icons can have different evidence. Antioxidant chemistry alone does not earn immunity, brain or longevity symbols. ⏰ is reserved for explicitly qualified aging research; 🕒 denotes circadian timing; 🎗️ identifies cancer-related evaluation without validating screening.
6. **Separate indication from administration details.** Tests are graded for clinical use, not merely analytical validity. Vaccines are graded for protection in eligible recipients, with schedule corrections listed separately. Equipment specifications, ordinary grooming preferences, historical surgery/vaccination dates, empty cells, composition panels and navigation links are not independent intervention claims.

Products, doses, medication schedules, portions, selected food options, timing and compartment allocation are preserved. Main's newer sweet-potato preparation/nutrition, PHGG and Boswellia entries are included. A medal does not grade purity, safety or organizer order. Status flags such as ⚕️, 🔄, ✅, FODMAP and staining markers keep their separate meanings in the [Legend](../README.md#legend).

### Scope of nutrient claims

**Nutrient provision** means contribution to established requirements and normal physiology; it can receive Gold without implying that more improves health in an already sufficient person. **A targeted intervention** is graded for the intended change, dose and population. This distinction follows the claim, not whether the ingredient appears inside a blend or in its own capsule. The ordinary micronutrient disclosures below concern provision; M.5 grades additional iron supplementation without established deficiency, and M.16 grades additional bone/vascular benefit from a high-dose vitamin-K combination. Their normal physiological roles remain well established. Relevant roles and intake limits are summarized in the [NIH nutrient fact sheets](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/list-all/).

### Environment and equipment

Water filtration, air filtration and monitoring receive **🥈 for the written exposure-reduction use**: benefit depends on contaminants, source control, room sizing and actual operation. Neither the highest CADR nor an ion-count specification establishes clinical superiority. HEPA evidence must not be transferred to ionization; ozone generation remains a separate concern. Hearing-exposure reduction and appropriate assessment of hearing changes receive **🥇 👂**; sudden hearing loss requires the existing urgent pathway. Fragrance-free laundry selection is **🥈** for reduced irritation in susceptible people. Claims of superior clinical outcomes from single-use organic face towels, certified-natural textile systems or bamboo toilet paper remain **🥉**. Certification can verify a material standard without proving fewer illnesses, acne lesions or longer life.

Knives, pans, dimensions, inventory quantities, textile orientation and manufacturer maintenance steps remain specifications. Remove the knife set’s **🛡️**: stainless steel is not an immunity intervention. Historical surgeries, dates, surgeons and reported recovery likewise receive no retrospective success medal.
