# Protocol emoji and evidence review

**Reviewed: 21 November 2026 · Protocol base: `bb1241d` · Canonical schedule: [README](../README.md)**

The annotations distinguish established uses, promising options and exploratory targets. Gold recognizes well-supported benefits at relevant doses, including useful symptom relief, performance, nutrition and specific physiological targets. It does not require proof of longer life or a trial of every retail product. The 21 November recalibration separates evidence strength from effect size and the extra benefit an intervention might add to this individual's existing stack.

This is a focused evidence audit of the displayed symbols and medals, including the nested ingredients, repeated food/supplement entries, test labels and procedures. It is not a systematic review or a validated GRADE assessment. Searches included primary human trials and new 2025–2026 publications, official product information, regulatory assessments and relevant trial records. Sources are linked beside the decisions. “No matching trial located” describes this search; it is not proof that no such study could exist.

## Decision rules

1. **Match the meaningful exposure.** Consider the active ingredient, amount, formulation, route, daily total and conditional schedule. Evidence can transfer between materially equivalent preparations without an exact-retail-product trial. Preserve differences that can change efficacy: probiotic strain, extract standardization, delivery system, fresh-root versus dry mass, or an inadequately studied dose.
2. **Grade the stated benefit.** A reproducible improvement in symptoms, function or an established physiological target can earn Gold even when modest. A supported homocysteine or triglyceride effect is graded as that marker effect; it does not need a cardiovascular-event trial unless event prevention is claimed. Target engagement alone, such as increased NAD or an aging-clock change, does not establish a clinical benefit. Report positive newer findings alongside null primary outcomes.
3. **Keep nutrition distinct from enhancement.** 🍎 and selected nutrient-role symbols describe normal physiology. Evidence for nutritional provision and protein anabolism can be strong while the added benefit in an already well-nourished person is small or uncertain. Nutrient essentiality alone does not justify a particular supplemental dose or establish a need for iron or high-dose vitamin K. Small culinary portions do not inherit concentrated-extract treatment effects.
4. **Make experiments legible.** 🧪 qualifies all clinical benefit targets in its row for the written use; it can stand alone when no specific benefit is established. A silver medal can coexist with it when related human evidence is supportive but application to this regimen remains exploratory. Absence of 🧪 is not proof that the exact SKU, every dose or this individual has been studied.
5. **Use meaningful tiers.** Gold means established support for the main stated benefit at a relevant dose/form; Silver means promising human support with meaningful limits in consistency, replication or applicability; Bronze means an exploratory benefit, preliminary/mixed evidence or a substantial exposure mismatch. Small effect size alone is not a Silver criterion. These are practical editorial judgments, not a validated certainty score, a safety rating or an estimate of personal marginal benefit.
6. **Grade blends by their principal supported purpose.** A blend can earn Gold when its main purpose is delivered by well-supported ingredients at effective doses. Missing finished-blend trials alone do not force Silver. The medal does not validate every secondary ingredient, prove synergy or establish extra benefit beyond other sources in the stack.

The **regimen is preserved**: product names and links, doses, daily totals, medication instructions, timings, order, preparation, conditional use and compartment allocation. The changes concern symbols, evidence medals and their explanation. A retained row or 🧪 marker does not establish safety; existing monitoring and clinical follow-up remain relevant. Food staining/FODMAP flags, shopping status and vaccination history are retained. Tests use symbols to identify what is assessed, rather than to claim that testing improves outcomes.

### How to read the revised symbols

Clinical targets describe outcomes supported by human research. **🧪 qualifies every clinical target in its row as exploratory** for the scheduled dose, formulation and population; it can stand alone when no narrower target is justified. Nutrient-role symbols describe normal physiology, without promising an extra benefit in a well-nourished person. Thus [vitamin D can retain bone and immune roles](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminD-HealthProfessional/), while ordinary B vitamins receive 🍎 rather than an energy or cognition enhancement claim. Blood and lipid markers use 🩸; glucose/insulin targets use 🍬. Improving a laboratory marker does not by itself establish fewer clinical events or longer life.

### Drinks and powders

**M.0.2 — Psyllium, 7.5g: 🥇 🚽 🩸.** Together with MicroVitamin+’s 2.5g, the daily amount is 10g. A [275-person IBS trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19713235/) found symptom benefit at 10g/day; a [24-week lipid trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9497178/) found lower LDL with 10.2g/day. These support digestive and lipid targets. A separate microbiome icon adds less clear clinical information, and a glucose claim is unnecessary for the documented use.

**M.0.3 — MicroVitamin+, 26g: 🥇 🍎 💪 🥊 🦴 👨‍🦳 🚽 🔄.** Gold describes its principal supported contribution: the [manufacturer’s formula](https://microvitamin.com/pages/microvitamin-plus-faq) supplies 5g creatine, meeting the [3g/day evidence condition for repeated high-intensity performance](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A02012R0432-20250820), and 2.5g psyllium toward the scheduled 10g/day. Ingredient evidence can support those uses without a trial of the complete mixture. It does not prove synergy, equal evidence for every constituent, or more benefit from the combined 10g creatine than from 5g. Other nutritional, physical and cosmetic domains retain their ingredient-specific qualifications below.

### Morning pills

**M.1 — Bifidobacterium longum 35624, 1bln CFU: 🥈 🦠 🚽.** The [pivotal randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16863564/) enrolled 362 women with IBS and found its clearest benefit at 100 million CFU, rather than the current billion-CFU dose. **M.2 — Gastrus, 200mln CFU: 🥈 🚽 🦠.** With the evening capsule, this supplies 400 million CFU daily. **M.4 — Ubiquinone, 100mg: 🥉 🧪.** Two recent trials make the original organ bundle difficult to defend. [Forty robust older adults receiving 400mg/day](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41604113/) gained plasma exposure without better mitochondrial, exercise or metabolic outcomes; a [111-person trial of 200mg ubiquinol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13404211/) did not establish overall cognitive or cardiovascular benefit. Neither proves that the current product is ineffective, but neither supports confident brain, heart, immune or longevity claims at 100mg.

**M.5-6 — Acetyl-L-Carnitine, 1.5g: 🥉 🧠 🧪 🔄.** A [56-person trial at 1.5g/day](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30906386/) improved one cognitive assessment in patients with vascular cognitive impairment. Keep cognition as a research goal, remove generic energy enhancement, and lower the medal.

**M.8 — Silicon, 14mg: 🥉 💇‍♂️ 👨‍🦳 🧪.** [The selected ORGONO product](https://hepatica.pl/produkt/krzem-premium-orgono-living-silica/) supplies orthosilicic acid in an acacia-based delivery system. A [50-woman skin trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16205932/) also used choline-stabilized OSA. Cosmetic targets remain plausible, but formulation equivalence is unproven. **M.9 — Lion’s Mane fruiting-body extract, 1g: 🥉 🧠 🧪 🔄.** Research remains active: an [October 2026 accepted trial](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2026.1904820/abstract) reported modest cognitive signals in 109 adults aged 40–75 using 2g of combined fruiting body and mycelial material. **M.10 — ErgoActive ergothioneine, 25mg: 🥉 🧠 👨‍🦳 🧪 🔄.** The [147-person branded trial](https://www.mdpi.com/1661-3821/5/3/15) tested 10mg and 25mg for 16 weeks in older adults with memory concerns. **M.11 — NAC, 1g: 🥉 🧪 🔄.** A [2026 trial in 59 older adults with possible vascular cognitive impairment](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13195-026-02062-z) found no cognitive advantage from 2.4g/day. ### Applying the evidence consistently

Mirror collagen, creatine and taurine decisions in the morning blend’s constituent rows. Treat magnesium taurate as a magnesium nutrient source; its taurate component is not interchangeable with the free-taurine trial doses. Apply the dutasteride change to its duplicate appearance.

The review considers newer positive findings, including intermittent dutasteride, tendon collagen, AstraGin, magnesium sleep outcomes and exploratory creatine cognition. It also keeps null primary outcomes, comparator results and formulation differences visible. A biomarker change, animal mechanism or registered study alone does not justify a human longevity, disease-prevention or organ-protection symbol.

## Maintenance and verification

The README is the annotation source of truth. Physical pill icons and medals are mirrored in `tools/generate_pillbox_guide.py`, and the generated guide is checked for all eight 3×3 pages, 72 physical compartments and 74 expanded entries. Repeated ingredients use the same interpretation across the schedule, diet summary and guide. Empty compartments and conditional-use warnings are preserved.

Future updates should revisit a badge when a dose, formulation, population/indication or substantive human result changes. Judge the main stated benefit consistently: established functional or physiological effects can earn Gold, while a new biomarker result or product label alone does not automatically justify a broader benefit icon. Record effect size, individual fit, extra benefit beyond the stack and safety separately from the evidence tier.
