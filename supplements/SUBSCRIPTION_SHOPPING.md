## How to read the prices

- **PLN unless explicitly EUR.** Prices are public product-page observations, not completed checkout quotes. A stock/add-to-cart indication is a snapshot, not reserved inventory. Recurring prices may change later.
- **30-day consumption cost** = pack price × protocol pills per day × 30 ÷ pack count. It uses the README dose, including morning plus evening duplicates, rather than the seller's recommended serving. This is not necessarily the amount charged every calendar month.
- **Supply days** = pack count ÷ protocol pills per day. Supply duration is a replenishment target, not proof that a merchant offers that delivery interval. Conditional products use actual use-days.
- The selection target is **free home courier and no more than 25% delivered-price premium**, following the protocol's shopping constraint. A **meaningful premium** is flagged at ≥10% or ≥10PLN per 30 consumption days. These are purchasing thresholds, not clinical effect thresholds.
- Tables show **goods-only** costs unless delivery is explicitly included. A comparison becomes a delivered comparison only when both actual baskets qualify for the stated shipping assumptions. Compulsory handling, small-order, membership and delivery fees belong in the comparison. Do not buy unwanted stock simply to reach a threshold.
- Welcome offers, unverified coupon codes, loyalty credit, cashback, expired offers and out-of-stock prices do not establish a sustainable recurring price. No Allegro Smart membership or free shipping is assumed.
