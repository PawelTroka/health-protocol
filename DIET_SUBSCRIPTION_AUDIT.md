## Quantity and delivery rules

- **Rotate choices within the written Diet.** 20g Nuts/day, 7g Morning Seeds + 7g Dinner Seeds/day, one Berry choice and one Whole Fruit choice are shared budgets. A subscription creates stock, not another daily serving; checked options remain the macro reference day.
- Quarterly Nuts total 1.8kg: 90×20g choices. HeyBio Flax 250g + Chia 200g leave 180g of the 630g Morning Seed budget for manual Hemp. A 200g Hemp pack therefore lasts about 100 days on average, with 20g carried forward if all three packs arrive together. Pumpkin 350g + Sunflower 250g supply 600g against 630g of Dinner Seeds; cover the 30g gap from existing stock and reduce these quantities if Tahini returns.
- Rolled Oats 2.3kg covers 92 days at 25g/day. The three Pulse packs give 53.97 cooked choices at an illustrative 2.4× yield, versus 51.43 at 4/week: omit one 400g Chickpea pack roughly every seven cycles, sooner with lower use or a higher actual yield. Keep the written 2–4 Pulse meals/week. Quinoa supplies about 20 Lunch choices, Potatoes up to 25 and monthly Orange Sweet Potatoes up to 32.1 per 90 days, leaving at least 12.9 Lunch choices for purple Sweet Potatoes; actual prepared yield controls.
- Fresh pack weights include varying peel, stalks and cooking losses. Use fresh deliveries in concentrated choices within the delivered use-by, then rotate other foods. **28/90 days is a reorder interval, not shelf life.** Potatoes, Celeriac, Avocado and Sauerkraut are quarterly rotation deliveries, with prompt use after arrival rather than 90-day refrigerated storage.
- Bioshi publicly offers 7/14/21/28/30/60/90-day presets and requires a shared interval per subscription order. Product-page subscription controls and supplier stock are weaker evidence than a valid final checkout. Carrot 84795 could not be found in active 357's add-product selector; the separate order route was verified in accepted order 243402, currently awaiting settlement.
- The former Fresh C test quoted 72.36zł goods + 9.49zł GLS = 81.85zł; it has been dismantled into F2/F4 membership. The new P2 draft quotes 133.34zł with free GLS, while the existing account groups also retain verified free delivery. These observations do not establish a universal subscription threshold or future renewal shipping. [Public delivery terms](https://bioshi.pl/pl/delivery) distinguish lockers and named couriers without explaining the observed GLS pricing; use each actual checkout.
- Confirm the precise SKU/form, quantities, automatic-payment disclosure, total and delivery before payment. Remove only matching duplicate manual purchases after the replacement is verified. Recalculate any lost grocery coupon or delivery threshold; do not add unused food to retain a discount.
- Annualized purchasing uses 365/90 = 4.06 quarterly cycles and 365/28 = 13.04 monthly cycles. A first order on day 0 can produce five 90-day charges in the first 365 days (days 0/90/180/270/360); the last delivery supplies the next period. Budget cash flow separately from consumption and carry stock forward.
- Product pages advertise 10% off from delivery 2; creation emails specify 10% for order 2 and 15% for orders 3–300. At the account check above, renewal quotes were **110.70zł for 357, 702.11zł for 359 and 239.38zł for 360**, each with free GLS. These are current account quotes, not future-price guarantees. Records 359/360 also request “Dodaj kartę i kontynuuj swoją subskrypcję”; an active label alone does not establish that automatic collection is ready. Use the actual renewal notice and verified payment setup. [Bioshi terms](https://bioshi.pl/pl/terms)
