A **reference** describes a comparison range; a **target** is a desired band. Clinical guidance, manufacturer comparisons, practical protocol targets and observational evidence are identified below. A color on a monthly mean describes that mean, not the number of abnormal nights or a diagnosis. September is month to date.

Stage percentages are **ratios of same-provider monthly means**, not means of nightly percentages. Body-component masses similarly use matched body mass; exercise combines matched intensity components. Matching requires identical observed-day coverage, proved by `observed_days` or a complete shared date interval. Missing components are never zero, and unmatched quantities remain ungraded.

Remaining contextual families include calorie totals, per-workout/session averages, exercise peaks/zones, goal settings, non-wear time, anatomical dimensions, time in bed/naps, respiratory extrema, proprietary breathing/movement fields without an established interpretation, maximum/session HRV, raw peripheral temperature, and Body Battery charged/drained flows. These do not inherit unrelated cutoffs. Unscored values are generally plain; PSA fractions retain ⚪. Categorical summaries remain counts, and provider definitions remain distinct.

## Reading trends

- Health-score trend dots retain their established calculation where comparable. Overlapping numeric bounds cannot establish a trend; identical bounds mean an unchanged reporting category, not equal measurements.
- Vitals trends use 🟢 for movement in the supported favorable direction and 🟡 for the reverse, even within one provider color band. Device scores use continuous higher/lower directions; target-based measurements use distance to the stated interval, without an invented midpoint optimum. Two values already inside the target have equal target distance and a ⚪ trend.
- Direction-only trends describe recovery, disruption or device-model change without inventing a numerical cutoff. ⚪ means equal target distance, no change at displayed precision, or context-dependent change; it does not automatically mean that the measurements are equal. New vitals trends do not claim a clinical effect size or minor/major severity.
- Calculations use the latest two nonmissing observations and displayed precision. Missing dates are skipped, so the previous observation may be more than one month earlier. A pending, inconclusive or uninterpretable latest result blocks an older comparison. One observation, fixed height, and unscored bounds or unrecognized categorical summaries have no trend.
- Recognized categorical summaries color each label, including Oura resilience and Garmin HRV status. Trends compare **proportions**, not raw day counts: an improvement requires no worse cumulative proportion at every ordered category boundary and a better proportion at least once; worsening reverses this rule. Mixed changes remain ⚪. Category codes are never averaged.
- Microbiota retains **↑ +value**, **↓ −value** and **→ 0** for numerical changes on the laboratory scale, without a health judgment. The ordered stool descriptions above can show a direction toward or away from “absent”, without a numerical delta.
- A table shows a Trend column only if a row has two comparable observations. Height is treated as fixed context. Trend appears immediately after the metric; empty month columns are omitted.
